<p>This page demonstrates how to create a curated list of relics by passing an array of specific relic IDs to the <code>createRelicListTemplate</code> function.</p>
<p>This is useful for creating specialized build guides or highlighting specific items of interest.</p>

<h3>Example List</h3>
<p>The following list displays only the relics with IDs 11, 12, 23, and 25.</p>

<%- await createRelicListTemplate(allRelics, allAffixData, { 
    keys: ['icon', 'name', 'eRelicSize', 'intrinsicAffixes'], 
    ids: [11, 12, 23, 25] 
}) %>
